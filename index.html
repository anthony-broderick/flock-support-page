Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions.

Flock uses CSV files for importing and exporting application data. Make sure you are using a .csv file and that the header row exactly matches the provided template. The header row must also be the first row of the file, with no additional rows or information above it.

No. You can use Flock's core application features without an account. An account is only required for AI-powered features, such as the Auto Resume Builder.

Go to Settings > Log in to an account > Build Profile. After completing your profile, create or edit an application and enter the company name, role, and job description in the notes section. Then select Generate Resume.

Flock uses a consistent resume template and builds your header and education section from the information in your profile. It then analyzes the job description and selects the skills, projects, and work experience that best match the position, generating relevant bullet points for each. You'll receive a preview of the resume that you can edit before downloading it.

After generating your resume, you'll be able to preview the PDF and make edits. Click on any bullet point field to change its text. To remove a bullet point, delete all of the text in that field. You can also remove an entire project or work experience entry by clicking the X next to its name. Once you are done, click the Finalize and Download button.

Your account stores your email address and the profile information you provide in the Build Profile section. Your password and job application information are not stored in the account database.

If your streak number appears incorrect, go to Settings and select Recalculate Streak. This will recalculate your streak based on your application activity.

Yes. Flock stores your application data locally, so applications do not automatically sync between devices. You can transfer your applications manually using the Export and Import features. If you have an account, your profile and subscription will carry over when you sign in on another device.
